[united-states] How Many Nuclear Weapons Does the US Have? 5,042 (2026)
  The United States has an estimated 5,042 nuclear warheads as of early 2026 (FAS). 1,670 are deployed on strategic launchers, about 100 more are deployed with allied or tactical aircraft, and 3,700 are in the military stockpile. A further 1,342 are retired and awaiting dismantlement. SIPRI’s January 2026 count matches.[1, 2]

[iran] Does Iran Have Nukes? Iran&#x27;s Nuclear Program in 2026
  No. As of early 2026, neither FAS nor SIPRI counts Iran among the world’s nuclear-armed states.[1, 2] The IAEA’s last stockpile estimate, from 13 June 2025, was 440.9 kg of uranium enriched up to 60%; since the June 2025 attacks the Agency says it cannot state the stockpile’s current size, composition or location.[3]
