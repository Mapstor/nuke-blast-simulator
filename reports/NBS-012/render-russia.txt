Home  /  Nuclear weapons by country  /  Russia    How Many Nukes Does Russia Have?
How many nuclear warheads  Russia  holds, what carries them, and its test and treaty record, as of early 2026. Two independent counts (FAS and SIPRI) are shown side by side, every figure cited.
By   Marko Visic, BSc Physics
Russia has an estimated 5,420 nuclear warheads as of early 2026 (FAS). 1,796 are deployed on strategic launchers, and 4,400 are in the military stockpile. A further 1,020 are retired and awaiting dismantlement. SIPRI’s January 2026 count matches. [  1 ,    2  ]

FAS and SIPRI are compiled by the same analysts and agree on the totals; they split the categories slightly differently.  Retired  warheads are awaiting dismantlement and are not part of the reserve.

Frequently asked questions
How many nuclear weapons does Russia have?
Russia has an estimated 5,420 nuclear warheads as of early 2026, according to the Federation of American Scientists. SIPRI’s January 2026 count is the same. [  1  ]
When did Russia first test a nuclear weapon?
Russia’s first nuclear test is recorded as test #1, 29 Aug 1949, STS (Semipalatinsk Test Site), surface, 22 kt. [  7  ]
Is Russia part of the Non-Proliferation Treaty?
On the NPT, Russia is: party (nuclear-weapon state); signed 1968-07-01; ratified 1970-03-05. [  10  ]
