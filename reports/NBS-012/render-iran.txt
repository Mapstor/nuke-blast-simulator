Frequently asked questions
Does Iran have nuclear weapons?
No. As of early 2026, neither the Federation of American Scientists nor SIPRI counts Iran among the world’s nuclear-armed states. [  1  ]
How much enriched uranium does Iran have?
The IAEA’s most recent estimate, as of 13 June 2025, was 440.9 kg of uranium enriched up to 60% U-235. The Agency says it cannot confirm the stockpile’s current size, composition or location. [  3  ]
Is Iran in the Non-Proliferation Treaty?
Yes. On the NPT, Iran is: party; signed 1968-07-01; ratified 1970-02-02; in force 1970-03-05. On the CTBT: signed 1996-09-24; not ratified. [  11  ]
Can the IAEA verify what Iran is doing now?
Not fully. The IAEA reports that its continuity of knowledge was lost for more than a year (since the June 2025 attacks), and that it is IAEA unable to discharge its NPT safeguards responsibilities in Iran. [  6  ]
