Home  /  Nuclear weapons by country  /  North Korea    How Many Nukes Does North Korea Have?
How many nuclear warheads  North Korea  holds, what carries them, and its test and treaty record, as of early 2026. Two independent counts (FAS and SIPRI) are shown side by side, every figure cited.
By   Marko Visic, BSc Physics
North Korea has an estimated 60 nuclear warheads as of early 2026 (FAS). FAS lists none as deployed and 60 held in reserve. SIPRI’s January 2026 count matches. [  1 ,    2  ]
Warhead count
FAS and SIPRI are compiled by the same analysts and agree on the totals; they split the categories slightly differently.  Retired  warheads are awaiting dismantlement and are not part of the reserve.

Frequently asked questions
How many nuclear weapons does North Korea have?
North Korea has an estimated 60 nuclear warheads as of early 2026, according to the Federation of American Scientists. SIPRI’s January 2026 count is the same. [  1  ]
When did North Korea first test a nuclear weapon?
North Korea’s first nuclear test is recorded as 2006-10-09 (announced by the DPRK; detected by over twenty IMS seismic stations). [  7  ]
Is North Korea part of the Non-Proliferation Treaty?
On the NPT, North Korea is: 1985-12-12. [  10  ]
