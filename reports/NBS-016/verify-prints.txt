Overview: Deep-penetration earthquake bomb (about 2.4 t Torpex fill) . With about 2.4 t (Torpex) of explosive fill (no published TNT equivalent) [ 1 ], the Tallboy is a conventional high-explosive weapon that releases its energy through chemical reactions, not nuclear processes. [ 2 ] Effect radii not shown The Tallboy is a conventional (non-nuclear) weapon. The nuclear blast and thermal scaling laws this site uses do not apply to chemical explosives, so no effect radii are shown. See the methodology page.
