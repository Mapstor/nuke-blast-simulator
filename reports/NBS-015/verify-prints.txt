================ NINE COUNTRY INTROS (with citation numbers) ================

--- russia ---
  Russia holds the world's largest nuclear arsenal, an estimated 5,420 warheads — about 44% of the world's roughly 12,187 warheads and more than any other country. It and the United States (about 5,042) together account for most of the warheads on Earth.[1,2]

--- united-states ---
  The United States has the world's second-largest nuclear arsenal, an estimated 5,042 warheads (about 41% of the world's roughly 12,187 warheads), and remains the only country ever to have used a nuclear weapon in war. Only Russia, with about 5,420, holds more.[1,2,3]

--- china ---
  China has an estimated 620 warheads and is expanding faster than any other nuclear power: about 1,000 operational warheads by 2030 (Pentagon, as quoted by FAS). That is still far below the United States (about 5,042) and Russia (about 5,420).[1,2,3]

--- united-kingdom ---
  The United Kingdom keeps a submarine-only deterrent of about 225 warheads — one of the smallest arsenals among the five NPT nuclear-weapon states, with a stated plan to raise its ceiling to 260.[1,2,3]

--- france ---
  France fields about 370 nuclear warheads across submarines and aircraft. With the United Kingdom, it holds one of the smallest arsenals of the five NPT nuclear-weapon states.[1,2]

--- india ---
  India has an estimated 190 nuclear warheads. It first tested a nuclear device in 1974 and again in 1998; with Pakistan across the border, it is one of two rivals that have never joined the Non-Proliferation Treaty.[1,2,3,4,5]

--- pakistan ---
  Pakistan has an estimated 170 nuclear warheads, slightly fewer than India across the border. Both countries tested in May 1998 and neither has joined the Non-Proliferation Treaty.[1,2,3,4,5]

--- north-korea ---
  North Korea has an estimated 60 assembled nuclear warheads and is the only country to have tested a nuclear weapon this century; its most recent test, in 2017, was its largest.[1,2,3,4]

--- israel ---
  Israel is estimated to hold about 90 nuclear warheads but has never confirmed a nuclear test or officially acknowledged its arsenal — a decades-long policy of deliberate ambiguity.[1,2,3]


================ IRAN 'Does Iran have nuclear weapons?' FIRST PARAGRAPH ================
  Every major open assessment places Iran outside the group of nuclear-armed states. It is a party to the Non-Proliferation Treaty[16] and has enriched uranium under its IAEA safeguards agreement, the stockpiles of which the Agency has measured and reported,[3] but it is not assessed to possess a weapon.
