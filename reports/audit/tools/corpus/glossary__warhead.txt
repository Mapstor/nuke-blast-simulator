In nuclear arsenals, "warhead" usually refers to the physics package on a delivery vehicle (ICBM reentry vehicle, SLBM RV, cruise missile, gravity bomb). Modern warheads are typically thermonuclear and yield 100-500 kt. The W76 and W88 are the most common US warheads; the B61 is a variable-yield gravity bomb.Related termsICBM (Intercontinental Ballistic Missile)A long-range ballistic missile (range > 5,500 km) designed to deliver nuclear warheads between continents.SLBM (Submarine-Launched Ballistic Missile)A ballistic missile launched from a submerged submarine, providing a survivable second-strike nuclear capability.MIRVMultiple Independently-targetable Reentry Vehicle — a single missile carrying several warheads, each able to strike a different target.See the full Nuclear Weapons Glossary.
