The 1 PSI light-damage radius extends to roughly 8.48 km, where windows shatter.What is the yield of the Trinity?The Trinity has a yield of 25 kilotons of TNT equivalent. That is 2× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb.Is the Trinity bigger than the Hiroshima bomb?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of approximately 15 kilotons. The Trinity at 25 kilotons is 2× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb.What thermal burn radius does the Trinity produce?Thermal radiation from the Trinity can cause 3rd-degree burns out to roughly 2.51 km and 2nd-degree burns out to 4.49 km in an air burst. Surface bursts reduce these radii by approximately 40 percent due to ground absorption.Sources: declassified DOE/DOD records, FAS, SIPRI, Glasstone & Dolan.
