Tactical (or "non-strategic") nuclear weapons include artillery shells, depth charges, short-range missiles, and gravity bombs. Modern tactical weapons like the B61 and Russian Iskander-K warhead have variable yields (sometimes called "dial-a-yield"). The line between tactical and strategic blurs at higher yields.Related termsStrategic Nuclear WeaponA nuclear weapon designed for long-range strikes against an adversary's population centers, industrial base, or strategic infrastructure.See the full Nuclear Weapons Glossary.
