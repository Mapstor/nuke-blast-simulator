About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the Fat Man (USA, 1945) detonated over Madrid (Spain). Nagasaki bomb.The capital of Spain and one of the largest metropolitan areas in the European Union. With an urban-core density of about 5,300 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The Fat Man delivers 21 kt of explosive yield — comparable in yield to the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 2.81 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 8.00 km. Given Madrid's urban density (~5,300/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 79,727 immediate fatalities and about 46,720 additional injured.How many people would die in Madrid from a Fat Man strike?An air burst of the Fat Man over Madrid could cause an estimated 79,727 immediate fatalities and 46,720 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 0.49 km) would kill approximately 3,999 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 1.32 km) would add 24,277; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 2.81 km) would add 51,451 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the Fat Man on Madrid?For an air burst over Madrid: fireball 0.49 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 1.32 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 2.81 km, light blast (1 PSI) 8.00 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 2.33 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~41 km from ground zero.Is the Fat Man bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
