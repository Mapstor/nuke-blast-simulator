A kiloton is the standard unit for tactical-yield nuclear weapons. The Hiroshima bomb was about 15 kilotons; modern artillery-fired nuclear shells (now retired) were sub-kiloton.Related termsYieldThe total energy released by a nuclear weapon, expressed in tons (or kilotons / megatons) of TNT equivalent.Megaton (Mt)A unit of nuclear yield equal to 1,000,000 tons (or 1,000 kilotons) of TNT equivalent.See the full Nuclear Weapons Glossary.
