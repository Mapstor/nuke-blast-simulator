About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the B61 (USA, 1968) detonated over Istanbul (Turkey). Variable yield tactical bomb.The largest city in Turkey and Europe's largest metropolitan area, straddling the Bosporus strait. With an urban-core density of about 2,900 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The B61 delivers 340 kt of explosive yield — 23× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 7.05 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 20.06 km. Given Istanbul's urban density (~2,900/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 274,054 immediate fatalities and about 160,599 additional injured.How many people would die in Istanbul from a B61 strike?An air burst of the B61 over Istanbul could cause an estimated 274,054 immediate fatalities and 160,599 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 1.49 km) would kill approximately 20,299 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 3.30 km) would add 76,896; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 7.05 km) would add 176,859 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the B61 on Istanbul?For an air burst over Istanbul: fireball 1.49 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 3.30 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 7.05 km, light blast (1 PSI) 20.06 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 7.31 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~124 km from ground zero.Is the B61 bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
