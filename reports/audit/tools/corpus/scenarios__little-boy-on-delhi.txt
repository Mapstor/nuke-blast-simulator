About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the Little Boy (USA, 1945) detonated over Delhi (India). Hiroshima bomb.The capital of India and second-largest metropolitan area in the world. With an urban-core density of about 11,000 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The Little Boy delivers 15 kt of explosive yield — comparable in yield to the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 2.52 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 7.16 km. Given Delhi's urban density (~11,000/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 132,517 immediate fatalities and about 77,657 additional injured.How many people would die in Delhi from a Little Boy strike?An air burst of the Little Boy over Delhi could cause an estimated 132,517 immediate fatalities and 77,657 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 0.43 km) would kill approximately 6,341 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 1.18 km) would add 40,657; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 2.52 km) would add 85,519 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the Little Boy on Delhi?For an air burst over Delhi: fireball 0.43 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 1.18 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 2.52 km, light blast (1 PSI) 7.16 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 2.03 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~35 km from ground zero.Is the Little Boy bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
