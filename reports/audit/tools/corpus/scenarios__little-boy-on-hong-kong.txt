About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the Little Boy (USA, 1945) detonated over Hong Kong (China). Hiroshima bomb.A special administrative region of China and one of the world's most densely populated territories. With an urban-core density of about 7,100 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The Little Boy delivers 15 kt of explosive yield — comparable in yield to the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 2.52 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 7.16 km. Given Hong Kong's urban density (~7,100/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 85,534 immediate fatalities and about 50,124 additional injured.How many people would die in Hong Kong from a Little Boy strike?An air burst of the Little Boy over Hong Kong could cause an estimated 85,534 immediate fatalities and 50,124 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 0.43 km) would kill approximately 4,093 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 1.18 km) would add 26,242; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 2.52 km) would add 55,199 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the Little Boy on Hong Kong?For an air burst over Hong Kong: fireball 0.43 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 1.18 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 2.52 km, light blast (1 PSI) 7.16 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 2.03 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~35 km from ground zero.Is the Little Boy bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
