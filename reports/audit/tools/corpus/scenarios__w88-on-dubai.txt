About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the W88 (USA, 1988) detonated over Dubai (UAE). Modern SLBM warhead.The most populous city in the United Arab Emirates and a global trade and tourism hub. With an urban-core density of about 950 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The W88 delivers 475 kt of explosive yield — 32× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 7.87 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 22.40 km. Given Dubai's urban density (~950/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 111,945 immediate fatalities and about 65,601 additional injured.How many people would die in Dubai from a W88 strike?An air burst of the W88 over Dubai could cause an estimated 111,945 immediate fatalities and 65,601 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 1.71 km) would kill approximately 8,689 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 3.68 km) would add 31,013; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 7.87 km) would add 72,243 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the W88 on Dubai?For an air burst over Dubai: fireball 1.71 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 3.68 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 7.87 km, light blast (1 PSI) 22.40 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 8.39 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~141 km from ground zero.Is the W88 bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
