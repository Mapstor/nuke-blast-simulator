About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the W88 (USA, 1988) detonated over Delhi (India). Modern SLBM warhead.The capital of India and second-largest metropolitan area in the world. With an urban-core density of about 11,000 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The W88 delivers 475 kt of explosive yield — 32× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 7.87 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 22.40 km. Given Delhi's urban density (~11,000/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 1,296,200 immediate fatalities and about 759,590 additional injured.How many people would die in Delhi from a W88 strike?An air burst of the W88 over Delhi could cause an estimated 1,296,200 immediate fatalities and 759,590 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 1.71 km) would kill approximately 100,608 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 3.68 km) would add 359,098; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 7.87 km) would add 836,494 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the W88 on Delhi?For an air burst over Delhi: fireball 1.71 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 3.68 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 7.87 km, light blast (1 PSI) 22.40 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 8.39 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~141 km from ground zero.Is the W88 bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
