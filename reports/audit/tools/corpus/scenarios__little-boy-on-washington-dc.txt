About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the Little Boy (USA, 1945) detonated over Washington DC (USA). Hiroshima bomb.The capital of the United States, home to the federal government, the Pentagon, and most major U.S. agencies. With an urban-core density of about 4,300 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The Little Boy delivers 15 kt of explosive yield — comparable in yield to the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 2.52 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 7.16 km. Given Washington DC's urban density (~4,300/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 51,802 immediate fatalities and about 30,357 additional injured.How many people would die in Washington DC from a Little Boy strike?An air burst of the Little Boy over Washington DC could cause an estimated 51,802 immediate fatalities and 30,357 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 0.43 km) would kill approximately 2,479 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 1.18 km) would add 15,893; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 2.52 km) would add 33,430 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the Little Boy on Washington DC?For an air burst over Washington DC: fireball 0.43 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 1.18 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 2.52 km, light blast (1 PSI) 7.16 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 2.03 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~35 km from ground zero.Is the Little Boy bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
