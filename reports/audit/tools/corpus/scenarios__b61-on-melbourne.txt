About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the B61 (USA, 1968) detonated over Melbourne (Australia). Variable yield tactical bomb.The second-largest city in Australia and capital of the state of Victoria. With an urban-core density of about 2,500 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The B61 delivers 340 kt of explosive yield — 23× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 7.05 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 20.06 km. Given Melbourne's urban density (~2,500/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 236,254 immediate fatalities and about 138,448 additional injured.How many people would die in Melbourne from a B61 strike?An air burst of the B61 over Melbourne could cause an estimated 236,254 immediate fatalities and 138,448 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 1.49 km) would kill approximately 17,499 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 3.30 km) would add 66,290; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 7.05 km) would add 152,465 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the B61 on Melbourne?For an air burst over Melbourne: fireball 1.49 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 3.30 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 7.05 km, light blast (1 PSI) 20.06 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 7.31 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~124 km from ground zero.Is the B61 bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
