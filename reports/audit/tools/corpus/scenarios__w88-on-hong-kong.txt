About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the W88 (USA, 1988) detonated over Hong Kong (China). Modern SLBM warhead.A special administrative region of China and one of the world's most densely populated territories. With an urban-core density of about 7,100 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The W88 delivers 475 kt of explosive yield — 32× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 7.87 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 22.40 km. Given Hong Kong's urban density (~7,100/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 836,638 immediate fatalities and about 490,280 additional injured.How many people would die in Hong Kong from a W88 strike?An air burst of the W88 over Hong Kong could cause an estimated 836,638 immediate fatalities and 490,280 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 1.71 km) would kill approximately 64,938 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 3.68 km) would add 231,782; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 7.87 km) would add 539,918 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the W88 on Hong Kong?For an air burst over Hong Kong: fireball 1.71 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 3.68 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 7.87 km, light blast (1 PSI) 22.40 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 8.39 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~141 km from ground zero.Is the W88 bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
