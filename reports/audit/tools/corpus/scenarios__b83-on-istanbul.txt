About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the B83 (USA, 1983) detonated over Istanbul (Turkey). Most powerful US weapon in active service.The largest city in Turkey and Europe's largest metropolitan area, straddling the Bosporus strait. With an urban-core density of about 2,900 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The B83 delivers 1.2 Mt of explosive yield — 80× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 10.69 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 30.41 km. Given Istanbul's urban density (~2,900/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 629,969 immediate fatalities and about 369,170 additional injured.How many people would die in Istanbul from a B83 strike?An air burst of the B83 over Istanbul could cause an estimated 629,969 immediate fatalities and 369,170 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 2.47 km) would kill approximately 55,671 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 5.00 km) would add 167,752; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 10.69 km) would add 406,546 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the B83 on Istanbul?For an air burst over Istanbul: fireball 2.47 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 5.00 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 10.69 km, light blast (1 PSI) 30.41 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 12.26 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~205 km from ground zero.Is the B83 bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
