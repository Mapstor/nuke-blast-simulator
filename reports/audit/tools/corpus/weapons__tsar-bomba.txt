The 1 PSI light-damage radius extends to roughly 104.12 km, where windows shatter.What is the yield of the Tsar Bomba?The Tsar Bomba has a yield of 50 megatons of TNT equivalent. That is 3,333× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb.Is the Tsar Bomba bigger than the Hiroshima bomb?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of approximately 15 kilotons.
