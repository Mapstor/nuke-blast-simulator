The 1 PSI light-damage radius extends to roughly 7.16 km, where windows shatter.What is the yield of the Little Boy?The Little Boy has a yield of 15 kilotons of TNT equivalent. That is comparable in yield to the Hiroshima bomb.Is the Little Boy bigger than the Hiroshima bomb?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of approximately 15 kilotons. The Little Boy at 15 kilotons is comparable in yield to the Hiroshima bomb.What thermal burn radius does the Little Boy produce?Thermal radiation from the Little Boy can cause 3rd-degree burns out to roughly 2.03 km and 2nd-degree burns out to 3.64 km in an air burst.
