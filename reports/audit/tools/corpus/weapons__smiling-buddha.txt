The 1 PSI light-damage radius extends to roughly 6.65 km, where windows shatter.What is the yield of the Smiling Buddha?The Smiling Buddha has a yield of 12 kilotons of TNT equivalent. That is 1.3× smaller than the Hiroshima bomb.Is the Smiling Buddha bigger than the Hiroshima bomb?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of approximately 15 kilotons. The Smiling Buddha at 12 kilotons is 1.3× smaller than the Hiroshima bomb.What thermal burn radius does the Smiling Buddha produce?Thermal radiation from the Smiling Buddha can cause 3rd-degree burns out to roughly 1.86 km and 2nd-degree burns out to 3.32 km in an air burst.
