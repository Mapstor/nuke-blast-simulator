The 1 PSI light-damage radius extends to roughly 8.48 km, where windows shatter.What is the yield of the Hurricane?The Hurricane has a yield of 25 kilotons of TNT equivalent. That is 2× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb.Is the Hurricane bigger than the Hiroshima bomb?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of approximately 15 kilotons. The Hurricane at 25 kilotons is 2× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb.What thermal burn radius does the Hurricane produce?Thermal radiation from the Hurricane can cause 3rd-degree burns out to roughly 2.51 km and 2nd-degree burns out to 4.49 km in an air burst.
