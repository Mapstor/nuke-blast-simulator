About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the W88 (USA, 1988) detonated over Sydney (Australia). Modern SLBM warhead.The largest city in Australia and host of the 2000 Summer Olympics. With an urban-core density of about 2,100 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The W88 delivers 475 kt of explosive yield — 32× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 7.87 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 22.40 km. Given Sydney's urban density (~2,100/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 247,456 immediate fatalities and about 145,013 additional injured.How many people would die in Sydney from a W88 strike?An air burst of the W88 over Sydney could cause an estimated 247,456 immediate fatalities and 145,013 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 1.71 km) would kill approximately 19,207 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 3.68 km) would add 68,555; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 7.87 km) would add 159,694 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the W88 on Sydney?For an air burst over Sydney: fireball 1.71 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 3.68 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 7.87 km, light blast (1 PSI) 22.40 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 8.39 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~141 km from ground zero.Is the W88 bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
