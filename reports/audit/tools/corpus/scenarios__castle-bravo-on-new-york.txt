About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the Castle Bravo (USA, 1954) detonated over New York (USA). Most powerful US nuclear test.The most populous city in the United States and a global financial capital, with a metropolitan area approaching 20 million people. With an urban-core density of about 10,000 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The Castle Bravo delivers 15 Mt of explosive yield — 1,000× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 24.60 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 69.98 km. Given New York's urban density (~10,000/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 11,504,873 immediate fatalities and about 6,742,002 additional injured.How many people would die in New York from a Castle Bravo strike?An air burst of the Castle Bravo over New York could cause an estimated 11,504,873 immediate fatalities and 6,742,002 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 6.79 km) would kill approximately 1,447,968 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 11.51 km) would add 2,632,315; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 24.60 km) would add 7,424,590 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the Castle Bravo on New York?For an air burst over New York: fireball 6.79 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 11.51 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 24.60 km, light blast (1 PSI) 69.98 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 34.54 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~562 km from ground zero.Is the Castle Bravo bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
