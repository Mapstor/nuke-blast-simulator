About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the W88 (USA, 1988) detonated over Jakarta (Indonesia). Modern SLBM warhead.The capital of Indonesia and largest city in Southeast Asia. With an urban-core density of about 16,000 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The W88 delivers 475 kt of explosive yield — 32× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 7.87 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 22.40 km. Given Jakarta's urban density (~16,000/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 1,885,382 immediate fatalities and about 1,104,858 additional injured.How many people would die in Jakarta from a W88 strike?An air burst of the W88 over Jakarta could cause an estimated 1,885,382 immediate fatalities and 1,104,858 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 1.71 km) would kill approximately 146,339 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 3.68 km) would add 522,325; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 7.87 km) would add 1,216,718 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the W88 on Jakarta?For an air burst over Jakarta: fireball 1.71 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 3.68 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 7.87 km, light blast (1 PSI) 22.40 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 8.39 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~141 km from ground zero.Is the W88 bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
