The 1 PSI light-damage radius extends to roughly 69.98 km, where windows shatter.What is the yield of the Castle Bravo?The Castle Bravo has a yield of 15 megatons of TNT equivalent. That is 1,000× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb.Is the Castle Bravo bigger than the Hiroshima bomb?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of approximately 15 kilotons. The Castle Bravo at 15 megatons is 1,000× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb.What thermal burn radius does the Castle Bravo produce?Thermal radiation from the Castle Bravo can cause 3rd-degree burns out to roughly 34.54 km and 2nd-degree burns out to 61.86 km in an air burst.
