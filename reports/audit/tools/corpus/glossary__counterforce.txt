Counterforce attacks target missile silos, submarine bases, airfields, and command bunkers. Success requires high accuracy and prompt strike capability — historically associated with the development of MIRVs and improved guidance. Counterforce is paired with first-strike scenarios and is destabilizing because it raises pre-emption incentives.Related termsCountervalueA nuclear targeting strategy aimed at destroying an opponent's population centers, industry, and economic infrastructure.First StrikeA nuclear attack intended to disarm an opponent before they can retaliate, by destroying their nuclear forces, command, and control.MIRVMultiple Independently-targetable Reentry Vehicle — a single missile carrying several warheads, each able to strike a different target.See the full Nuclear Weapons Glossary.
