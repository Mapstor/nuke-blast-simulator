About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the W88 (USA, 1988) detonated over Tokyo (Japan). Modern SLBM warhead.The capital of Japan and the world's largest metropolitan area; Greater Tokyo holds over 37 million people. With an urban-core density of about 6,300 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The W88 delivers 475 kt of explosive yield — 32× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 7.87 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 22.40 km. Given Tokyo's urban density (~6,300/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 742,369 immediate fatalities and about 435,038 additional injured.How many people would die in Tokyo from a W88 strike?An air burst of the W88 over Tokyo could cause an estimated 742,369 immediate fatalities and 435,038 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 1.71 km) would kill approximately 57,621 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 3.68 km) would add 205,665; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 7.87 km) would add 479,083 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the W88 on Tokyo?For an air burst over Tokyo: fireball 1.71 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 3.68 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 7.87 km, light blast (1 PSI) 22.40 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 8.39 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~141 km from ground zero.Is the W88 bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
