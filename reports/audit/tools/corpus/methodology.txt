Scientific MethodologyBlast Effect CalculationsThe simulator uses scientifically validated scaling-law formulas derived from nuclear weapons testing data and the seminal work The Effects of Nuclear Weapons by Glasstone & Dolan (3rd edition, 1977). All formulas express radius as a function of weapon yield in kilotons.Fireball RadiusRadius (km) = 0.28 × Yield0.33The fireball vaporizes everything within this radius.
