About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the B83 (USA, 1983) detonated over Tokyo (Japan). Most powerful US weapon in active service.The capital of Japan and the world's largest metropolitan area; Greater Tokyo holds over 37 million people. With an urban-core density of about 6,300 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The B83 delivers 1.2 Mt of explosive yield — 80× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 10.69 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 30.41 km. Given Tokyo's urban density (~6,300/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 1,368,554 immediate fatalities and about 801,990 additional injured.How many people would die in Tokyo from a B83 strike?An air burst of the B83 over Tokyo could cause an estimated 1,368,554 immediate fatalities and 801,990 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 2.47 km) would kill approximately 120,940 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 5.00 km) would add 364,427; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 10.69 km) would add 883,187 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the B83 on Tokyo?For an air burst over Tokyo: fireball 2.47 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 5.00 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 10.69 km, light blast (1 PSI) 30.41 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 12.26 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~205 km from ground zero.Is the B83 bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
