About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the B61 (USA, 1968) detonated over Mexico City (Mexico). Variable yield tactical bomb.The capital of Mexico and the largest city in North America by population, with a metro area of over 21 million. With an urban-core density of about 6,000 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The B61 delivers 340 kt of explosive yield — 23× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 7.05 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 20.06 km. Given Mexico City's urban density (~6,000/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 567,007 immediate fatalities and about 332,274 additional injured.How many people would die in Mexico City from a B61 strike?An air burst of the B61 over Mexico City could cause an estimated 567,007 immediate fatalities and 332,274 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 1.49 km) would kill approximately 41,997 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 3.30 km) would add 159,096; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 7.05 km) would add 365,914 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the B61 on Mexico City?For an air burst over Mexico City: fireball 1.49 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 3.30 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 7.05 km, light blast (1 PSI) 20.06 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 7.31 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~124 km from ground zero.Is the B61 bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
