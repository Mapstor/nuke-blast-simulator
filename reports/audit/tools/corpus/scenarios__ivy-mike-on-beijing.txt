About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the Ivy Mike (USA, 1952) detonated over Beijing (China). First hydrogen bomb test.The capital of the People's Republic of China and one of the most populous cities in the world. With an urban-core density of about 1,300 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The Ivy Mike delivers 10.4 Mt of explosive yield — 693× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 21.80 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 62.01 km. Given Beijing's urban density (~1,300/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 1,174,484 immediate fatalities and about 688,263 additional injured.How many people would die in Beijing from a Ivy Mike strike?An air burst of the Ivy Mike over Beijing could cause an estimated 1,174,484 immediate fatalities and 688,263 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 5.86 km) would kill approximately 140,429 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 10.20 km) would add 276,110; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 21.80 km) would add 757,945 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the Ivy Mike on Beijing?For an air burst over Beijing: fireball 5.86 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 10.20 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 21.80 km, light blast (1 PSI) 62.01 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 29.72 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~485 km from ground zero.Is the Ivy Mike bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons. The Ivy Mike at 10.4 Mt is 693× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb.Casualty math uses Beijing's urban-core density and the scaling laws on the methodology page.
