Surface bursts produce massive fallout because the fireball touches the ground, vaporizing soil and irradiating it with neutron flux. The radioactive debris is lofted into the upper atmosphere and drifts downwind, depositing as a long elliptical plume. Air bursts produce minimal fallout because the fireball does not interact with the ground.
