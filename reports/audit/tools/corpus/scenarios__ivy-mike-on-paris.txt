About this scenarioThis page calculates what would happen if the Ivy Mike (USA, 1952) detonated over Paris (France). First hydrogen bomb test.The capital of France and one of the most densely populated major cities in Europe; the Île-de-France region holds over 12 million people. With an urban-core density of about 21,000 people per km², even a relatively small detonation over the city center would affect a large population.The Ivy Mike delivers 10.4 Mt of explosive yield — 693× more powerful than the Hiroshima bomb. The 5 PSI moderate-blast zone — where most residential buildings collapse — would extend to 21.80 km. Light blast damage and shattered windows would reach 62.01 km. Given Paris's urban density (~21,000/km²), this scenario yields an estimated 18,972,426 immediate fatalities and about 11,118,084 additional injured.How many people would die in Paris from a Ivy Mike strike?An air burst of the Ivy Mike over Paris could cause an estimated 18,972,426 immediate fatalities and 11,118,084 additional injuries. The fireball alone (radius 5.86 km) would kill approximately 2,268,464 people; the severe-blast zone (20 PSI, radius 10.20 km) would add 4,460,238; the moderate-blast zone (5 PSI, radius 21.80 km) would add 12,243,724 more. Real numbers depend heavily on time of day, sheltering, weather, and altitude of detonation.What is the blast radius of the Ivy Mike on Paris?For an air burst over Paris: fireball 5.86 km, severe blast (20 PSI) 10.20 km, moderate blast (5 PSI) 21.80 km, light blast (1 PSI) 62.01 km. Thermal radiation causes 3rd-degree burns out to 29.72 km. A surface burst would shrink the blast radii by roughly 40 percent but generate massive radioactive fallout extending ~485 km from ground zero.Is the Ivy Mike bigger than the bomb that hit Hiroshima?The Hiroshima bomb (Little Boy) had a yield of about 15 kilotons.
