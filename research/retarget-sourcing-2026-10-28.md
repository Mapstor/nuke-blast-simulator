## E. Nuclear explosion (four-kill article, retargeted)
- State (Glasstone & Dolan, 1 Mt unless noted): an explosion is "the very rapid release of a large amount of energy"; the energy comes from fission or fusion, sustained by a fission chain reaction; energy split 50% blast, 35% thermal, 5% initial radiation, 10% residual; the fireball forms in under a millionth of a second, 440 ft across at 0.7 ms and 5,700 ft at 10 s; light and heat travel at the speed of light; initial radiation is that emitted in roughly the first minute; the shock front is 3 miles ahead of the fireball at 10 s and 12 miles out at 50 s, moving at about 1,150 ft/s; the cloud reaches 3 miles at 30 s, 5 miles at about 1 minute, and peaks after about 10 minutes.
